| D-026 | 2026-10-09 | **Tenancy = Postgres RLS for real.** The app connects as the `flowledger_app` login (member of the `flowledger_app_role` NOLOGIN group: not owner, not superuser). Policies on workflows, executions, logs, integrations, api_keys and audit_events, plus public-read for public workflows. | D-031 | 2026-10-09 | Vault: AES-256-GCM, a per-workspace data key wrapped by versioned master keys `VAULT_MASTER_KEY_V<n>` (falls back to `INTEGRATION_ENCRYPTION_KEY` as v1).
